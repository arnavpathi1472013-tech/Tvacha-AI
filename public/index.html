About & privacy

TVACHA AI is a professional skin analysis AI.

Photos are resized in your browser, sent over HTTPS for one analysis, and never saved by this app or cached offline. No analytics are used.

To keep the free service fair, a daily usage counter is kept against a one-way hash of your network address. It does not include your photo or answers.
